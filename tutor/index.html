How it works

Three simple steps

No packages to buy up front and no long-term commitment. Start with one session and see how it goes.

  1. 1

    Reach out

    Send a short note with your student's grade, class, and what feels hardest right now. I'll reply within a day with a few time options.

  2. 2

    Meet & map it out

    The first session is a friendly assessment. We find the gaps, set a goal, and agree on how often to meet.

  3. 3

    Practice & progress

    Each week we build on the last. You'll get a brief note after every session on what we covered and what to review.
